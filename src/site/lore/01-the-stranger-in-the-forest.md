Hands drag you out of the water.

![A forest stream](story:ch1-1)

You cough, and the world comes back in pieces: a stream running over smooth rocks, roots and moss, old trees so tall the light comes down in thin green threads. Here and there, a squared stone pokes out of the earth, as if something was built here once.

> "Hey! Are you still breathing?
> What happened? Why are you lying out here?"

!["Hey! Are you still breathing?"](story:ch1-3)

## The stone

![A stone with a strange mark](story:ch1-2)

Something lies on the bank beside you. A single stone, small enough to close your hand around, worn smooth with age. Carved into it is a strange mark.

You don't know what it means. But when you look at it, something in you *almost* remembers.

> **???**
> *An ancient stone with some kind of pattern carved into it.*

## A roof and a fire

![A villager's hut](story:ch1-4)

You wake again under a thatched roof, a fire crackling nearby.

> "You're lucky. If we'd come any later..."

They ask your name. Where you come from. Why you were lying in the middle of the forest. You try to answer, and find there is almost nothing there. Only blurry images. And the stone.

![Questions you can't answer](story:ch1-5)

## The road to the city

Once you can walk, the villagers take you with them. The road follows the water: fields, boats, a village built along the river, people who live by its rhythm.

![The road to the village](story:ch1-6) ![Life along the river](story:ch1-7)

Then the trees open up, and you see it for the first time: a great walled city of towers and canals, the heart of this land.

![The city, for the first time](story:ch1-8)

At the gate, soldiers stop you.

![The city gate](story:ch1-9)

> "No travel papers...
> You'll have to register at the hall first."

So you are written down as a newcomer. A stranger with no past.

![Registered as a newcomer](story:ch1-10)

## A city of waterways

The city is built on water and faith. Canals run between tiered spires, and boats carry people and goods out to the lands beyond. In the crafts quarter, smiths and traders call out to every newcomer. At its centre stands a great sacred hall, and at night a beam of light rises from it into the sky.

![The crafts and trade quarter](story:city-trade) ![The great sacred hall](story:city-hall) ![The waterway harbour](story:city-harbour) ![The residential quarter](story:city-homes)

## The questions

The stone is still in your hand: the only thread tying you to a story you've forgotten.

![The only thread](story:ch1-11)

A new life begins here. Work, people, roads to walk and monsters on the roads. And the questions don't go away.

![A new life begins](story:ch1-12)

*Who are you?*
*Why can't you remember?*
*And why does that stone feel like it's yours?*
