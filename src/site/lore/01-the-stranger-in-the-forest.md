title: The Stranger in the Forest
summary: You wake again, among roots and moss, with a stone you almost remember.
---

You open your eyes. Again.

This time there are no arches, no voices, no incense. Only trees: old, tall, packed so tightly that the light comes down in thin green threads. Moss covers everything. Here and there, a squared stone pokes out of the earth as if something was built here once.

You try to remember how you got here. You can't. Your own name feels far away.

## The stone

Something lies in the leaves beside you. A stone, small enough to close your hand around, worn smooth with age. Carved into it is a pattern.

You don't know what it means. But when you look at it, something in you *almost* remembers.

> **???**
> *An ancient stone with some kind of pattern carved into it.*

## The village

You walk until the trees thin out. A villager on the path stops, stares, and then hurries over. You look like someone who has been lost for a very long time.

They take you home. There is food, a fire, a bed. There is a village that doesn't know who you are either.

When the village elder sees your stone, they turn it over in their hands for a long moment.

> "I've never seen a mark like this."
>
> "But this stone is far older than you."

## The questions

A life starts here: work, people, roads to walk and monsters on the roads. Somewhere along the way, a strange creature begins to appear at your side. It seems to know the way. It seems to want you to grow stronger.

And the questions don't go away.

*Who are you?*
*Why can't you remember?*
*And why does that stone feel like it's yours?*
