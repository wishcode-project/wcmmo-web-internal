In a fight you shouldn't have to look around the screen to know if you're in trouble. So the HUD puts everything that matters in **one compact block at the bottom centre**, in a wooden style that matches the world.

## The main block

| Where | What you see |
|---|---|
| Left panel | your **health**, as numbers and a red bar. The border turns silver while you guard |
| Right panel | your **mana**, as numbers and a blue bar |
| Centre | your **level** in a hexagon that fills with your **stamina**: green, then yellow, then red. While you sprint the number turns into a running icon |
| Below | a segmented **XP bar** |
| Above | the **five skills** of your active bar with their keys and cooldowns, a `1/2` badge for which bar is active, and your weapon's **ultimate** ringed by the Remnant gauge. When it glows and shows **Q**, it's ready |
| Next to your health | your **Bloodline's** signal: Fury glows red at the screen edge, Ward shows its shield pips, Pulse shows its rhythm beats |
| Bottom | your item hotbar in a wooden frame |

The vanilla hearts, food bar, armour and air bubbles are gone. The HUD replaces them.

## The rest of the screen

- **Top left:** party frames for up to four members, with their health and Bloodline
- **Top centre:** the boss health bar, and the zone name when you enter a new area
- **Top right:** your current quest objective, and your buffs and debuffs below it
- **Above monsters:** name, level and health

## Readable in both languages

Everything on the HUD will be readable in Thai and English, including Thai vowels and tone marks at every GUI scale. We're checking that carefully, because pixel fonts usually don't have Thai letters.

*This is the first layout. It will change once we see it in game.*
