We had a full design built around classes. This week we threw it out.

Classes lock you into one weapon and one role from minute one. We want you to be able to pick up an axe because it looks cool, and have that actually matter.

## Your Bloodline is your identity

Instead of a class, you carry **one Bloodline**. It doesn't hand you a pile of stats. It changes *how you play*, and it evolves as you grow. The first one we're building is the **Berserker**: the closer to death you get, the more dangerous you become.

## Runes let you tune it

Runes are small passives you slot to shape your build. Same Bloodline, different Runes: one player builds a wall, another a glass cannon.

## Any weapon, if you can handle it

Stats decide which gear you can **equip**, not how hard you hit. Every weapon type has its own unique skills, and you unlock them by **mastering** that weapon in combat.

## Why this matters

Every system has one job, so no single grind makes you unbeatable:

- your stats let you equip gear
- enhancement makes you stronger
- mastery makes you faster and unlocks skills
- your Bloodline and Runes decide your style
