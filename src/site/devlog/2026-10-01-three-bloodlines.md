In the last devlog we said goodbye to classes and promised a Bloodline instead. Back then we only had one rough idea, the Berserker. Since then the design has grown up: there are now **three base Bloodlines**, and every new player starts with one of them.

You still don't pick it from a menu. The Awakening decides which one is in tune with you.

## Fury: the harder you're hit, the harder you hit

Fury turns your health into a resource. The lower it drops, the faster and more dangerous you get. At its peak, Fury can pay with its own blood to reset every skill cooldown, and throw itself straight back in.

- **Plays like:** pushing forward, never backing off
- **Best at:** clearing fast
- **Watch out:** one mistake can end the fight

## Ward: guard, store, release

Ward rewards patience. Every hit you block from the front builds up your defence, and a guard timed at the last moment can stagger the attacker. Store that power, then spend it: stand immovable, or turn it into one crushing blow. Later, bone-white armour lets you soak a storm of hits.

- **Plays like:** staying calm and reading the enemy
- **Best at:** surviving anything
- **Watch out:** it starts slow; you have to let them swing first

## Pulse: keep the rhythm

Pulse is about flow. Chain *different* skills one after another and every third one releases a pulse that heals you and hurts everything around you. Repeat the same skill and the rhythm breaks. Pulse is **not** a healer class: it links to enemies by default and drains them, and helping an ally is an option, not a job.

- **Plays like:** a steady, clean rotation
- **Best at:** long fights
- **Watch out:** no big burst, and spamming one skill kills the rhythm

## Eight builds each

Every Bloodline grows through five stages as you level. The first and fourth stages are its identity. At the **second, third and fifth** stages you choose one of **two paths**: one leans towards survival, the other towards damage. That's eight builds per Bloodline before you even pick a weapon or slot a single rune, and you can change a path later.

## Solo first

We had one rule for all of this: **every Bloodline must work on its own.** Each one can recover, raise its damage, and survive a mistake without anyone else. Anything that helps allies is a bonus on top, never the point. And every Bloodline works with every weapon: melee, ranged or magic.

## More to come

These three are the **base** Bloodlines: good at everything, extreme at nothing. New Bloodlines will arrive with the story, each one a specialist with its own new way to play, and you'll earn them by playing, not by buying them.

*All numbers are starting values and will change a lot once we can test them. Next up: proving the combat tech, and the first real footage.*
