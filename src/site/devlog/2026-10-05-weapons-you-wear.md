Last week we said the controls would keep changing as we tested. They did. After trying three-click combos, per-weapon loadouts and a few other ideas on the test server, this is where combat stands. It replaces the controls in Devlogs #3 and #8.

## Animated weapons

Every weapon is now an animated model, not a flat item. It hangs on your back while you walk, run and jump, and when you fight it swings with its own moves, slash trails and hit effects. Our first test set is made of **wood**: a bow, a spear, swords, a shield and a two-handed staff that fires a magic beam.

## Weapons you wear, not hold

You don't hold weapons in your hotbar any more. You **wear** them. A new **equipment screen** has slots for your armour, wings, accessories and two weapons, **right hand** and **left hand**, with your normal inventory underneath.

**Tap Shift** and your weapons come off your back into your hands. Tap again and they go back. Nothing is moved between slots, so nothing can be lost or dropped by accident.

## Stances

What you wear in the two hands decides how you fight:

| Right hand | Left hand | Stance |
|---|---|---|
| sword | empty | single sword |
| sword | sword | dual swords |
| sword | shield | sword & shield |
| spear, bow or staff | empty | two-handed |

A two-handed weapon needs the left hand empty. Staves will get the same choices as swords later: one staff, two staves, or staff and shield.

## The controls now

| Input | Does |
|---|---|
| Tap Shift | combat mode on / off |
| Left click | basic attack, bows too |
| Right click (tap in time) | parry |
| Right click (hold) | guard, or a full block with a shield |
| 1–5 | cast your skills |
| Shift + right click | switch to your second set of five skills |

Your skills are still yours, not the weapon's: you pick ten, five on each set. Hotbar slots 1–5 always show your skill icons, and slots 6–9 are free for potions and food. The three-click combos are gone.

## Tiers and skins

Every weapon will come in three looks as it gets stronger: **wood**, **iron** and a **master-crafted** look. A **skin** changes how a weapon looks and nothing else. One sword can wear any sword skin, and your stats stay the same.

## What's next

*All of this is prototype work and will keep changing as we play it.*
