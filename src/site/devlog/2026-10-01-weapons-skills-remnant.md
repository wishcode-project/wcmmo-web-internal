Your Bloodline decides *how* you fight. Your weapon decides *what* you can do. Here's how the weapons and skills fit together.

## Eight weapons, four to start

There are eight weapon types: **Sword, Greatsword, Hammer and Spear** up close, **Bow and Crossbow** at range, **Staff and Tome** for magic. The first playable version has the **Sword, Hammer, Bow and Staff**; the other four follow.

Anyone can pick up any weapon if they have the stats for it, and every weapon can be played solo from start to finish.

| Weapon | Feels like |
|---|---|
| Sword | fast, combos, counters |
| Hammer | slow and heavy, smashes guards open |
| Bow | long range, shots fire instantly, keep moving |
| Staff | big area spells |

## Five skills and an ultimate

Every weapon has **five skills of its own**, unlocked one by one as your **Mastery** with that weapon grows. The sword starts with Blade Flurry and works up to Thousand Cuts; the hammer goes from Ground Smash to Titan Fall. Each weapon also has **one skill that breaks an enemy's guard**, and the Hammer breaks almost anything.

At high Mastery you unlock the weapon's **ultimate**: *Blade Storm* for the sword, *Earthbreaker* for the hammer, *Heaven's Barrage* for the bow and *Cataclysm* for the staff.

## The Remnant gauge

Ultimates don't have a cooldown. They have the **Remnant**, a gauge that fills while you fight: every hit, every skill that lands, and every blow you take pushes it up, and it never drains while you wait. At 100, press **Q** and let it go. You're immune to being knocked around while an ultimate plays out.

## General skills and Skill Orbs

On top of the weapon kit you carry **general skills** that work with any weapon: Dash, Backstep, Sidestep, Kick, Leap, Battle Cry and more. You learn them in the tutorial, from level-ups, and from trainers in the cities.

Then there are **Skill Orbs**. Buy one from the Orb Merchant or find one on a monster, use it, and you learn an extra skill: some work with a whole weapon family (any melee weapon, say), some only with one weapon. They're about as strong as a mid-tier weapon skill, never stronger than a weapon's best.

## Ten slots, two bars

You fit all of this into **ten skill slots**: two bars of five. Keys **1–5** cast the skills on the active bar while you hold a weapon, **F** swaps bars mid-fight, **Shift** guards, and **Q** fires the ultimate. General skills, weapon skills and orb skills all compete for the same slots, so what you bring is a real choice.

*Skill names and numbers are our current plan and will be tuned once we can test them.*
