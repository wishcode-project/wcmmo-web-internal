# 029 — HUD layout

> Status: DRAFT · Target: wcmmo (MythicHUD layout, Nexo pack for HUD art, kit placeholders) · FIRE mode: confirm
> Design: GDD v2 · Reference: [boards/hud-reference.png](../gdd/boards/hud-reference.png) (a screenshot of another game's HUD, used only as a style reference; our art is our own) · Decisions: D-01, D-39, D-60, D-61

## Big picture

- **Player story:** Everything I need in a fight sits in one compact block at the bottom of the screen, in a wooden, readable style: HP on the left, Mana on the right, my level in the middle with my stamina around it, my XP under it, my skills above it and my items below it.
- **Done means:** the layout below renders with MythicHUD, reads the kit placeholders, hides the vanilla hearts / food / armour / air / XP bar, and stays readable at GUI scale 2–4.

## Bottom-centre block (the main HUD)

┌─────────────────┐   ⬡    ┌─────────────────┐
│ 2395 / 2395   ♥ │  48   │ 137 / 137     ● │   ← HP (left) · level hex · Mana (right)
│ ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰ │ ▓▓▓▓  │ ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰ │   ← hex fill = stamina
└─────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘
 ▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱ EXP ▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱   ← XP bar (segmented)
 [🗡][  ][  ][  ][  ][  ][🧪][  ][📖]            ← vanilla hotbar, wooden frame

| Part | Shows | Rules | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HP panel (left)** | `current / max` + red bar + heart icon | panel border turns **silver while guarding** | vanilla health · `%wcmmo_guarding%` |
| **Mana panel (right)** | `current / max` + blue bar + mana icon | — | `%mmocore_mana%`, `%mmocore_max_mana%` |
| **Level hex (centre)** | the **level number** | while **sprinting** the number becomes a **running icon**; back to the number when you stop | `%mmocore_level%` · `%wcmmo_center_mode%` (`level` / `sprint`) |
| **Hex fill = Stamina** | the hex fills with stamina | **green ≥ 60 %**, **yellow 30–60 %**, **red < 30 %** (the running icon blinks when red) | `%wcmmo_stamina_pct%`, `%wcmmo_stamina_color%` |
| **XP bar** | segmented bar under the panels | fills with XP % of the current level | `%wcmmo_xp_pct%` |
| **Skill bar** | 5 icons of the **active bar** above the panels, key numbers 1–5, cooldown overlay, badge `1/2` | swaps with **F** | `%wcmmo_skillbar%` + MMOCore cooldowns |
| **Ultimate** | the held weapon's ultimate icon at the left end of the skill bar, ringed by the **Remnant** gauge; glows + shows **Q** at 100 | locked icon if Mastery < 25 | `%wcmmo_remnant%` |
| **Bloodline badge** | small emblem left of the HP panel + its mechanic | Fury: red edge glow when active · Ward: 5 Bulwark pips · Pulse: 3 rhythm beats + Bond thread | `%wcmmo_bloodline%`, `%wcmmo_fury_adrenaline%`, `%wcmmo_ward_bulwark%`, `%wcmmo_pulse_rhythm%`, `%wcmmo_pulse_bond%` |
| **Hotbar** | vanilla item slots in a wooden frame | slots 6–9 = items (D-60) | vanilla |

**Hidden vanilla parts:** hearts, food, armour, air, XP bar and level number. **Kept:** hotbar (restyled), chat, tab list, crosshair.

## Controls tied to the HUD (D-60)

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **1–5 while holding a weapon** | cast the matching skill of the active bar (the held item doesn't change) |
| **6–9** | select item slots (potions, food, spare weapon) |
| F | swap bar 1 ↔ 2 (spec 007) |
| Q | ultimate (spec 025) |

⚠️ Keys 1–5 depend on MMOCore's skill casting mode: PoC-3 confirms it can keep the weapon in hand while 1–5 cast.

## Rules

1. **One message channel:** kit messages ("Skill bar 2", "Remnant not full", countdowns) must go through a **MythicHUD notification element**, not the vanilla action bar, or they flicker against the HUD. The kit's `send action bar` calls are switched once the HUD is live (VERIFY how MythicHUD takes messages).
2. **Readable at every size:** check GUI scale 2, 3 and 4, and 16:9 / 21:9 / 4:3 windows; nothing overlaps chat or the hotbar.
3. **Art:** HUD textures and fonts are ours, merged into the Nexo pack (D-40). Wooden panels, pixel style like the reference.

## Vertical slice

Must have: HP / Mana panels, level hex with stamina fill + sprint mode, XP bar, skill bar with cooldowns and `1/2`, ultimate + Remnant, Bloodline badge, boss bar, party frames, quest tracker.
Later: zone name + AP/DP, custom buff icons, day / night themes.
