> **Direction locked by the story team:**
> Tutorial = the past → the Selection Stone shatters → the present: the player wakes in the same place, now a forest → a stone fragment carved with their Bloodline lies beside them → a villager rescues them → they reach the town → they start searching for their own past.

## 1. The core idea

| | |
|---|---|
| Main mystery | Not only *"which Bloodline am I?"* but *"who am I?"*, *"why can't I remember?"*, *"what did I have to do with what happened centuries ago?"*, *"why did the stone choose me?"* |
| Theme | Chapter 1 asks **"Who am I?"** Chapter 2 asks **"If I was that person, who will I choose to be in this life?"** The game never asks what you were born as. It asks what you **choose** to become. |
| Bloodline's role in the story | The Bloodline is a **clue**, not the answer. |
| Rule of reveal | The player should recognise things **from their own play**, not from an NPC reading lore to them. |

## 2. The hidden truth (what really happened)

⚠ TEAM ONLY

1. **Long ago, the player's past self tried to unite every Bloodline in one body**, to go beyond human limits.
2. **It failed.** They were defeated. Their **body and soul were torn apart** and **all memory was lost**.
3. **Pieces of their soul survived as fragments.** In the present, these fragments appear to the player as helpers: they teach, grant power, and guide the player towards parts of the truth.
4. **The fragments are the player.** The stronger the player grows, the more the fragments rejoin, and the more memories return.
5. The person the player has been searching for all along **is themselves**. The one who tried to unite the Bloodlines was **not a saviour but the cause of the old catastrophe**.

### The Selection Stone (ศิลาคัดสรร)

- In the tutorial (the past), a great **Selection Stone** reacts to the player and chooses their Bloodline (see spec 024).
- Then **something happens** that we do not explain: an explosion, a strange light, the ground shaking, or someone saying one key line. **The stone shatters.** Cut to black.
- In the present, the player wakes with a **fragment broken off in that moment**. It is not just debris: it is a piece that split away **during the past event**.
- This raises the question at once: *if the tutorial happened centuries ago, why is the player still here?* This is the **main mystery** of the game.

## 3. Reveal ladder (what the player knows, when)

| When | Player sees / learns | Player should think |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter 0 (tutorial) | A complete, living place: people, buildings, events. Trials. The Stone chooses them. Then the event and the shattering | "This is the tutorial / the starting area." Nothing says it is the past |
| Chapter 1 start | Wakes in a dense forest, remembers almost nothing. An old stone with a faint carving lies beside them | Dream? Memory? Vision? Another world? Leave it open |
| Chapter 1 early | NPCs react to the stone: *"ลายแบบนี้...ข้าไม่เคยเห็น"* (I've never seen a mark like this) / *"แต่หินนี่เก่ากว่าเจ้ามากแน่"* (but this stone is far older than you) | "The stone matters and it's old" |
| Chapter 1 middle | Back in the forest on a quest: the stream, the cliff, a huge tree where the plaza was, wall ruins that match the buildings | "...did the devs reuse the map?" 😂 |
| Chapter 1 middle-late | A **large broken stone base**. The hollow in its centre has **the same shape as the stone they carry** | "Wait..." |
| Chapter 1 late | The spirit companion **knows too much** about the player's past | Unease |
| Chapter 1 boss | The companion reveals itself as **a side of the player's old mind** | "It was me all along" |
| Later main quest | Murals / records / ruins confirm the tutorial place **really existed** | "It really happened" |
| Final reveal | Tutorial map = the same area today. The player can stand on **the exact spot of the game's first shot** and see what it has become | The big "เชี่ย..." moment |
| Later chapters | The full truth: they tried to unite all Bloodlines and caused the catastrophe | Chapter 2+ theme |

**Do not reveal early that the tutorial is the past.** Let players interpret it several ways first.

## 7. Map & level design requirements

The payoff only works if the player **recognises the place by themselves**.

| Landmark | Tutorial (past) | Present (forest) |
|---|---|---|
| Stream | same course | same course, overgrown banks |
| Cliff | same shape | same shape, vines |
| Plaza | open plaza | a huge old tree grows in its centre |
| Buildings / shrine walls | intact | wall ruins that line up with the old buildings, half buried |
| Selection Stone | whole, on its base | **broken base**, hollow in the centre matching the fragment |
| First shot | the camera spot of the game's first moment | reachable; the player can stand on it and see what it became |

**Production rule:** build the tutorial and the present-day forest **from the same base terrain** (copy it, then add buildings for the past and overgrowth for the present), so every landmark matches exactly.

## 8. Writing rules

- Don't explain. Hint, then let the player's own memory do the work.
- Early NPCs only react to what they see ("never seen this mark", "older than you").
- The companion is warm and helpful through most of Chapter 1.
- Player-facing text is gender-neutral and goes through translation (Thai + English).
- Keep the reveal ladder order; any new quest that reveals something must say which rung it is.
